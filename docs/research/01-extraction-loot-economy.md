Research 01 — extraction-genre loot economy and loss design
===========================================================

Sources read: [Ludo.guide, Tarkov insurance system](https://www.ludo.guide/guide/escape-from-tarkov/the-ultimate-survival-guide/gear-inventory-management/4-13-insurance-system-explained);
[Guild Order, insurance and loss recovery](https://guildorder.com/games/tarkov/wiki/insurance-and-loss-recovery). Read 2026-11-07.

What the genre does
-------------------

- Two insurers at two price and reliability tiers; insured gear left on a corpse comes back after hours to days unless
  another player extracted with it. Insurance is sold as risk management: it lets players run expensive kits and
  learn maps aggressively.
- Items returned by insurance lose their found-in-raid status, so the premium status is tied to a single clean
  extraction. Quest and some rare items cannot be insured at all.
- Loss is therefore graded: cheap kit is disposable, mid kit is insured, top kit is insured and prayed over, and the
  quest-critical item is carried bare.

Decisions this changed in the design
------------------------------------

1. **Spored tags are the insurer, with one tier and one rule.** A Bench craft from Spore Sac tissue regrows the tagged
   item at the Heart once, after a shift, and the regrown item loses found-in-run. Two tiers would add a menu, one tier
   keeps the decision on the item card (Keep / Spore / carry bare).
2. **The Mat is the free insurer for the ground it owns.** Items dropped on Mat tiles come home by themselves next
   shift. This makes "fight on the Mat" a real tactical choice and is the lore's version of a safe zone.
3. **Found-in-run is lost on regrowth and on appraisal-by-fence**, so the Corpo premium stays a reward for the clean
   extraction and the clean appraisal, as in the genre.
4. **Corpses are lootable by raiders on a daily roll**, so recovery runs are urgent and the route card says so.
5. **Keys and Relics cannot be Spored**, matching the genre's uninsurable category; losing a Vault card is a story.
