Research 06 — Ivan Ooze, the inspiration's look and voice
=========================================================

Source read: [Wikipedia, Mighty Morphin Power Rangers: The Movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mighty_Morphin_Power_Rangers:_The_Movie). Read 2026-11-07.
The film itself is the visual reference; the article covers role and plot.

What the canon gives
--------------------

- A shapeshifting ("morphological") being who once ruled Earth, played as a ferociously funny villain; he sells his
  ooze as a children's toy and uses it to control the parents; he fields Oozemen and Tengu warriors and digs up twin
  war machines. The film's design is a purple-skinned, ridged, robed figure, and the ooze is purple.
- He is defeated by being lured into the path of a comet.

Decisions this changed in the design
------------------------------------

1. **The Mat is purple ooze, not green mycelium.** SEEPAGE v2 replaces the green ramp with the Ooze ramp
   (2A1140 / 5A1E8C / A44BE0 / F0A6FF) and Ivan is its saturated end with Hot Pink rim light. Green survives only as
   sewer water and mould.
2. **"Child's Ooze Toy" is a Curio** (street set), the toy-jar from the film re-read as a pre-vaccine Night City object.
3. **Ivan's voice is theatrical and funny first**, cruel second: bark pools are written as a showman who finds the
   Oathkeepers absurd, which matches both the canon performance and the lore's framing monologue.
4. **Ivan's minions are not his**: the sewerats are lost symbiotes, nobody's army, which keeps the game's slimos as
   people rather than Oozemen. The one echo is the Spore Sac, an organ that produces what he cannot command.
5. **The name stays swappable**: the narrator's name is a string-table entry (`data/strings.toml`, `narrator.name`), and
   the Python project in this repository is already called `irwins-ooze`; nothing mechanical depends on the name.
