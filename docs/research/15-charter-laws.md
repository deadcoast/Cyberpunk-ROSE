Research 15 — laws under pressure (the Charter)
===============================================

Sources read 2026-11-09: [Frostpunk: Adaptation, the Book of Laws (gamepressure)](https://www.gamepressure.com/frostpunk/adaptation/zdad7a);
[How Frostpunk's laws and Hope system work (macmyths)](https://macmyths.com/how-frostpunks-laws-and-hope-system-work/);
the design's own source, `docs/02/Pass 2 (pillars).md` (The Charter, Unrest, Pillar 5's Dissolution row, Pillar 6's
Authority). The Frostpunk wiki's Book of Laws page carried no rules.

What the genre does
-------------------

- Frostpunk's laws sit in trees with prerequisites ("after introducing") and mutually exclusive pairs (Soup or Food
  Additives; Cemetery or Corpse Disposal); signing starts a cooldown that varies by law (the community wiki gives
  36 h for one and 72 h for another, version unconfirmed); several laws create a promise to build, and an unmet
  promise displeases the people. Hope falling low and staying there is a defeat condition.
- Laws act on Hope and Discontent both at signing and while in force.

Decisions this changed in the design
------------------------------------

1. **No signing cooldown.** Pass 2 already paces the Charter with a currency that never comes from scrap
   (Authority from deeds), aiming at one law per two to three hours; a cooldown on top would be a second gate on
   the same thing. A district formed is the deed available in Phase C, and a first law costs one Authority, so the
   first district pays for the first law inside Pass 2's 20–40 minute beat.
2. **No exclusive pairs and no prerequisite tree at launch.** Pass 2's eight laws have none; the eight touch
   different systems, and a pair would remove a choice Pass 2 leaves open. Prerequisites can arrive with chapter
   laws (Tithe is already gated by its event).
3. **The promise is the petition.** Frostpunk's build-promise is Pass 2's foreman petition at Mood 10: the city
   names the law or the structure, and a refusal has a counted consequence (twice dissolves the district) where
   Frostpunk's is a mood penalty.
4. **Repeal exists** (the design's Charter screen lists it) and is free but refunds nothing, so a law signed and
   repealed costs its Authority and the shifts its wave took to arrive and leave.
