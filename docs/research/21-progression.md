Research 21 — research, blueprints and the organism's progression
=================================================================

Sources read 2026-11-11: [RimWorld wiki, Research](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Research) (points per pawn, the
bench factors, project costs, techprints), [Dead Cells wiki, Cells](https://deadcells.wiki.gg/wiki/Cells) (blueprints
found in play, turned in, paid for with a currency lost on death), with the design's own: Pass 2's Pillar 6 (the tree's
three branches and four tiers, the classes, the population tiers, Authority), Pass 1's Genome (the ten genes, the five
organs and their lines, reflection at 0 and 1), the Full Design's Progression table (the pacing targets).

What the genre does
-------------------

- RimWorld's research is points from a pawn at a bench a game hour: speed linear in the Intellectual skill (8 % at 0,
  100 % at 8, 238 % at 20), the bench a multiplier (a simple one × 0.75). Projects cost 300–4,000 points by tech level,
  so a project is one to ten days of one researcher. Some projects ask a techprint first — an item found or bought —
  before any point counts.
- Dead Cells gates its unlocks on blueprints that drop in play and are paid for in cells at the Collector; the cells
  are lost on death, so what is found in a run is at risk until it is home.

Decisions this changed in the design
------------------------------------

- A blueprint opens a branch's tier, as a techprint opens a project, and is spent studying it: research is loot
  (Pass 2) and a blueprint in a rig is lost with its Runner, as Dead Cells' cells are.
- Points a shift are the researcher's Tweak level, linear as RimWorld's. Costs are set from the pacing targets, not
  copied: an hour is about five shifts, so a tier-2 node is 24 points (six shifts of one Tweak-4 slimo), tier 3 48,
  tier 4 96, and the three nodes of a tier take one researcher 18, 36 and 72 shifts — tier 2 by shift 15, tier 3 by
  40, tier 4 by 75 with a second researcher from tier 3.
- The Research job asks Tweak 2, not 5: a D3 is born at Tweak 2 and Tweak XP came only from crafts, so Tweak 5 put
  the first project past the tier-2 target (and Tweak 3, tried first, left `rose city`'s D3s out of it for twenty
  shifts). Research (2 a shift) and Build (1 a shift) now give Tweak XP.
- Tier 1 is the founding's kit and stands researched: Pass 2's first ten minutes use Pits, Bait, Pods and the Bench.
- Twelve organs, one for each gene Pass 1 left without one and a second for Chrome Affinity and Conductance, so any
  feed plan grows something; each grows by itself at the richest eligible Node, capped at 1 + Nodes / 30 of a kind.
- Genes reflect at 0 and 1 as Pass 1 wrote; the code had clamped them. Reflection is how a stable strain loses its
  class to one feed too many, and each gene's reflection is a Strainbook card.
